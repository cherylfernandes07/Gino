Sign ministry is a form of evangelism where people go to sporting events (or any kind of event you could imagine) and display placards of scripture verses, such as Mark 8:36, Romans 10:9, I Thes.4:16, Matt 11:28, and especially John 3:16. Or maybe something as simple as Jesus saves. What ever the conviction, it is a simple message on a sign, displayed peacefully at an event. Many opportunities to preach the Gospel arise at these events too. Preaching and handing out tracts is encouraged.
Ultimately the goal is; "Trust in the Lord with all your heart. In all of your ways acknowledge him and he will direct your paths".
This means not only trusting the Lord during the process, but to also trust him with the results, that we sometimes can't completely comprehend. "For we walk by faith and not by site"!
