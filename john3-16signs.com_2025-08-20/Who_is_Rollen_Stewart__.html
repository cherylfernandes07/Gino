Who is Rollen Stewart ?


Rollen Stewart is the original sign man. AKA Rainbow man, Rocken Rollen. God gave him the sign ministry in 1980. It took special skill and wisdom that God equipped him with, and gave him a zeal for, and calling to do. He is temporarily grounded but I'm believing in the God of restoration, that he wants to restore Rollen, and that good things are going to come from his story. A story where the end has yet to be written.
